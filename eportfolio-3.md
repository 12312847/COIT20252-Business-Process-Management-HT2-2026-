## Artefact 3 – Process cybersecurity in the Week 8 lecture

<p align="center"><img src="assets/cyber_controls.png" alt="Confidentiality, integrity and availability controls for a proposed assignment check" width="900"></p>

*Figure 3. Security controls considered for the submission workflow (adapted from CQUniversity 2026, pp. 24–28).*

**Source:** [Week 8 lecture](assets/week8_lecture.pptx), slides 24–28.

**Summary:** The lecture relates process security to confidentiality, integrity and availability, and discusses controls such as limiting access, monitoring and testing (CQUniversity 2026, pp. 24–28). Even a small automated check needs access to files and accounts.

**Reflection and justification:** My earlier model showed completion steps but not access rights. I selected these slides to examine that missing part: a bot with broad GitHub or Moodle permissions might increase risk. I would limit it to the files needed for checking, keep passwords out of the script and review its output. If these controls cost more effort than the check saves, I would keep the manual step.
